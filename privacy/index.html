Astervolt Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Contact. For anything about your data, use the developer email on Astervolt's Google Play page.

What we collect

  • Player ID: a random code created the first time you play online. It is not linked to your name, email or phone.
  • Pilot name: the name you type. It is shown publicly on the leaderboards, so please don't use your real name.
  • Scores: your best Endless and Chaos scores and when you reached them.
  • Technical data: your IP address and basic device and browser information, which our server provider logs automatically for security for a short time.

Your game progress (gold, upgrades, skins, settings) is stored only on your device. We don't receive it.

We don't collect your real name, email, phone number, location or contacts, and we don't sell data. The game shows no third-party ads.

Why, and on what legal basis

  • To run your account and the leaderboards (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b), needed to provide the game).
  • To keep the service secure and stop cheating and abuse (Art. 6(1)(f), our legitimate interest).

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase (database and sign-in), servers in Ireland (EU). Supabase acts under a data processing agreement. Where data may be accessed from outside the EU, it is protected by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and/or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Google (Google Play), under Google's own terms.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account, or 12 months after you last played online, whichever comes first. Server logs are kept for a few days.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, object to processing, or restrict it. You can also complain to your national data protection authority.

  • Change your name: Settings → Pilot → CHANGE.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete account. This deletes your player ID, name and scores from our server immediately.

Delete your data without the app

Email the developer address on Astervolt's Google Play page with the subject "Delete my data" and your Player ID (shown in Settings under your pilot name). We delete your account and scores within 30 days and confirm by email.

Children

Astervolt is not directed at children under 13. If you learn that a child gave us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will update this page when the game changes what it collects, and change the date at the top.
